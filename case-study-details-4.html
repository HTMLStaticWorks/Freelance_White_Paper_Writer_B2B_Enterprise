The Challenge

Enterprise prospects were stalled in the "analysis paralysis" phase of their cloud migration. The client needed to prove they had the technical depth to handle massive legacy datasets.

The Solution

I developed the "Enterprise Cloud Migration Blueprint"—a 35-page guide that detailed the exact sequence of data migration for heterogeneous legacy environments.

The framework was used by the client's solutions architects as a pre-sales assessment tool, directly resulting in a significantly faster technical approval process from prospect IT teams.
