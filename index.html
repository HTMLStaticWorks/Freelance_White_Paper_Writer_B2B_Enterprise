Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about our research and writing process.

What is your typical turnaround time?

+

For a standard 15-25 page white paper, the typical timeline is 4-6 weeks. This includes discovery, research, drafting, and two rounds of technical refinement.

Do you conduct primary research?

+

Yes. Every flagship report includes dedicated SME interviews and analysis of internal or public datasets to ensure the content provides unique value to your audience.

Can you help with the graphic design?

+

While my core focus is strategy and writing, I partner with specialist B2B designers to provide full-service report production, ensuring your paper looks as professional as it reads.

What industries do you specialize in?

+

I specialize in high-complexity B2B sectors, primarily Fintech, Cybersecurity, SaaS, and Enterprise Infrastructure, where translating technical detail into commercial authority is critical.

How do you ensure technical accuracy?

+

Accuracy is paramount. I work closely with your technical leads and engineering teams through structured interview sessions and rigorous peer-review cycles before any draft is finalized.
